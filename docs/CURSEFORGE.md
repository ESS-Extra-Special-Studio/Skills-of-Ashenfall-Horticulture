## Description

Horticulture is the second Skills of Ashenfall skill for RuneScape: Dragonwilds, levels 1 to 25. It is hidden until you earn it.

**Unlocking.** Reach Historian 25 and Farming 25, then find the Annotated Hymnal by the wild cabbage patch near the Wise Old Man in Bramblemead Valley and read it. Below Historian 25 the second script between its lines can't be made out. Once read, Horticulture appears in your skills menu and on character select, and the reading itself takes you to level 2.

**Training.** Sowing, watering, composting, weeding, curing blight and harvesting pay XP when the plot actually changes, so a failed attempt pays nothing. The first sowing of each kind of crop pays extra.

**Discovery Catalogue.** The first harvest of each of 24 crops is catalogued, with a card and bonus XP. End shows your level and the catalogue; Shift+End rereads the Observances of Brassica Prime.

**Multiplayer.** Each player's Horticulture is their own. The hymnal is placed on each player's machine and never saved into the world.

### Install

1. Install UE4SS for RuneScape: Dragonwilds (3.0.1, the "UE4SS Steam (latest)" build).
2. Install ESL:DragonWilds and Skills of Ashenfall: Historian (both Required Dependencies).
3. Install Horticulture. All three folders sit side by side in `RSDragonwilds\Content\Paks\~mods` and ship with `enabled.txt`.

## Lore and affiliation

**MOD LORE.** The Observances of Brassica Prime are fan-written, not Jagex canon. What they lean on comes from Dragonwilds' own journal: Brassica Prime, Guthix and the druids sowing cabbage across Ashenfall, the fried cabbage and the failed cabbage wine. The author, the scraped hymnal and the Observances themselves are invented for this mod.
